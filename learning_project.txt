HOW TO READ THIS PLAN
  * Each week runs Tuesday -> Monday. The deadline is the Monday it ends on.
  * Sub-tasks are study units, not calendar days -- do them in whatever order
    fits your week, but finish all of them (and the deliverable) by the deadline.
  * Hours are estimates for a focused learner. If a week runs long, cut the
    optional sub-task rather than the deliverable -- the deliverable is what
    proves the week's material stuck.

HOW THE 16 WEEKS FIT TOGETHER
  Phase 1  Weeks 1-4    Foundations: syntax, control flow, data structures
  Phase 2  Weeks 5-8    Working code: files, errors, modules, OOP
  Phase 3  Weeks 9-13   Depth: generators, testing, APIs, databases
  Phase 4  Weeks 14-16  Real project: packaging, async, capstone

  Note on Weeks 12-13: these land on the winter holidays (Dec 22 - Jan 4).
  They are deliberately lighter -- review and consolidation rather than new
  material -- so a disrupted week doesn't derail the plan.
